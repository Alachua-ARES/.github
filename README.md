# Alachua County ARES®

**Amateur Radio Emergency Service volunteers serving Alachua County, Florida.**

We are licensed amateur radio operators who train to provide backup emergency communications for Alachua County, its shelters and its Emergency Operations Center when normal systems fail.

## Get involved

- **Website:** [nf4rc.club](https://www.nf4rc.club)
- **Email list:** [groups.io/g/NF4RC](https://groups.io/g/NF4RC)
- **Monthly meeting:** 2nd Wednesday, 7 PM (in person and on Zoom; details on the email list)
- **Alachua ARES net:** Thursdays, 8 PM, on the GARS K4GNV 146.820 MHz repeater. Open to anyone who wants to check in.
- **No license yet?** Visitors are welcome at meetings, and we can point you to local license classes.

## Who we are

Alachua ARES is part of the ARRL Amateur Radio Emergency Service program in the ARRL Northern Florida Section.
Our club call signs are **NF4RC** (North Florida Amateur Radio Club) and **NF4AC** (Alachua EOC Radio Club).

Most of this organization's repositories are private and visible only to members.
Members: switch to the **member view** at the top of this page.
